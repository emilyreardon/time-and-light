# time-and-light
A series of 4×4 inch ceramic tiles made from cyanotypes of my garden — propagated from my aunt's, which came from my late mother's, grown alongside my late grandmother's and the gardens of her sisters-in-law. Installed in the house my family landed in during lockdown, meant to be temporary, now permanent.
The arrangement traces matrilineal propagation: plants and people, generational time and the variations carried in DNA.
Alongside the tiles, animated GIFs draw from the same archive: women, gardens, cyanotype prints looping on your phone while you stand at the wall. A living record of who tends, and carries growth forward.
